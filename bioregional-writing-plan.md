# Bioregional writing plan

Started 2026-10-12. A checklist for turning the Cambridge chapter ("Bioregional Design: Rethinking Furniture Production Through Local Systems and Regeneration") into wiki tiddlers and short essays, one section at a time.

## How to use this

- One small item a day. One essay a week. Check the box when the tiddler exists, even if it is rough.
- Each week follows one section of the chapter, so the reading and the writing stay in the same place.
- Skip around freely. Nothing here depends on order except the items marked **needs Justin**.
- To get a head start on a week, tell Claude: "skeleton week 3". Claude writes the factual shell for the notes and chairs (maker, year, materials, source link, tags) and leaves the claim to you.
- To get a draft to react to, tell Claude: "draft [title]". Expect to rewrite it.
- *(started)* means Claude wrote a factual shell on 2026-10-12. Your part is to read it, fix it, and add what the thing argues. Check the box when it sounds like you.

**The one rule:** write every piece fresh, from your notes and interviews. Don't paste sentences from the chapter. The ideas, facts, and interviews are yours to reuse. The chapter's wording is the publisher's.

**Kinds of item**

- `note` — a concept or reference tiddler, 50 to 150 words
- `chair` — a `Chair-ness` entry: who, when, what it is made of, and one line on what it argues. Tag by era and by cycle (`Biological Nutrient`, `Technical Nutrient`, `Monstrous Hybrid`)
- `essay` — 500 to 1,000 words, starting from the concrete case named

New work gets the tag `Bioregional Design`, to keep it apart from the `SDCSC` chapter drafts.
