# People Processing Plastic
## Scene V — The Object

*MARA moves to the mold. A sheet press. She places the processed material inside. Applies pressure. Time passes — real time, unhurried. The machines do their work.*

*THE SHEET speaks for the first time. Unhurried. Self-possessed. It has known what it was going to become for a long time.*

**THE SHEET:** I am almost ready.

**THE SHEET:** I have been a mountain and an ocean and a bottle and a problem and a fragment and a flake and a melt and now—

**THE SHEET:** I am deciding what to be next.

**THE SHEET:** A table. A tile. A chair. A surfboard. A wall panel in a building someone will live in for forty years.

**THE SHEET:** They will not know I was a bottle.

**THE SHEET:** I will not mind.

*THE MICROPLASTIC speaks one last time. The voice is different now — not fragmented, not waiting. Something settled. Something that has found its direction even if it hasn't yet found its way entirely home.*

**THE MICROPLASTIC:** I see what is possible now.

**THE MICROPLASTIC:** I have always been made of the right stuff.

**THE MICROPLASTIC:** There was just no door.

**THE MICROPLASTIC:** Build the door.

**THE GUEST:** How long does this take?

**MARA:** Depends what you're making. This — a few minutes. Something structural, longer.

**THE GUEST:** And then it can be recycled again?

**DEV:** And again. And again. That's the point. There's no finish line. There's no end of the story. Just the next form.

**THE GUEST:** Forever?

**MARA:** Long enough that forever is the right word to use.

*MARA opens the mold. Removes a small finished piece — a tile, a coaster, a small panel. Multicolored, marbled, smooth, unmistakably beautiful. She holds it for a moment. Then hands it to THE GUEST.*

**THE GUEST:** *(turning it over, genuinely moved)* I want this.

**MARA:** Good.

**THE GUEST:** I mean — I really want it. More than something new.

**DEV:** That's the moment we're working toward. Every time.

**THE GUEST:** What do I owe you?

**MARA:** Bring us your plastic. Separated. Clean.

**THE GUEST:** That's it?

**MARA:** That's the whole economy.

*THE BOTTLE speaks for the last time. Or the first time. Depending on where you are in the circle.*

**THE BOTTLE:** I was made this morning.

**THE BOTTLE:** I will be made again tomorrow.

**THE BOTTLE:** The word for what I am is not waste.

**THE BOTTLE:** The word for what I am is—

**THE BOTTLE:** next.
