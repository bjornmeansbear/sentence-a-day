[GD3] Project 2: stop motion

Create a stop motion animation. This will can be any simple combination of photo stills, or anything else—Use drawing, cut paper, physical objects, people, whatever … Create a series of motions—or a single engaging narrative—that represent Aristotle’s philosophy of “physics” that we discussed in class, as well as utilize the various kinds of change you'll be reading about in <cite>GD: New Basics</cite>.

Show me that you get the concepts of Aristotle’s Ideas of Motion, the basic kinds of change, and the various time + motion topics we have covered thus far. Please also prove that you have a grasp on design elements such as form, layout, and color. As long as you accomplish these things you are free to do whatever you wish content wise. 

Cleverness need not play too large a role in your piece—the banal or mundane is fine as long as you achieve the goals of the assignment—good design has never had to be fancy. There isn't much time, so craft in the making will be more important than amazing, groundbreaking ideas or techniques—but remember, Design should be, at some level, conceptual + abstracted. Try cut paper, or random objects, or even vector illustrations … see what you can combine: Photos, Video Stills, hand drawn elements, Found objects, etc.

We are not trying to make beautiful films — we are trying to use stop motion animation to explore the more basic concepts of motion — you don't need to be overly clever, too fancy, or use sound — just make some good, solid motion design.

--

Here are some things to think about:
<em>Structure/Composition</em>: how is the screen space being used
<em>Image/Image type</em>: what is the nature of the imagery, what is being implied
<em>Symbols & Symbol types</em>: What symbolic forms are being used
<em>Time</em>: how is time being used?
<em>Intent</em>: What is the collective intent of all elements?
<em>Meaning</em>: What is the collective meaning of all combined elements?
