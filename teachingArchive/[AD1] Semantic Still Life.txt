[AD1] Semantic Still Life

Part 1.
In preparation for your senior project explorations... create a still life with 1-15 objects that symbolically represent your influences, inspirations, and needs as a designer. Each object should be chosen for a reason. In a separate document, make a list of the items and the significance of each one. 

Part 2.
Condense your still life down to a flag. Is this 1 beautiful shape on a field of color? what other types of imagery or visual simplification can you use that try to convey the same message, feels like it goes with your still life picture, but can be successful as a flag?

Part 3.
“Good Graphic Design is something you can explain to someone over the telephone”
— Bob Gill
Write clear instructions for making your flag. We will try making each others in class.

Deliverables:
- Photographic Print
- Final Size 10x16in
- List of objects and their significance
- Flag
- Instructions for someone to make your flag
- Examples of others having made the flag...
