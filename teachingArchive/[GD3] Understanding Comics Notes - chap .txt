7 main types of word/image combinations:
1. Word Specific - image is illustrate what the words are saying
2. Image Specific - words basically add a "soundtrack" to what the images are showing you.
3. Duo-Specific - words/images basically send the same message
4. Additive - words/images amplify or elaborate on the other
5. Parallel - words/images follow different courses without intersecting
6. Montage - words sort of become integral parts of the picture 
7. Interdependent - words + pictures go hand in hand to convey an idea that neither could alone.
In general, the more one does the lifting, the more the other can be free to explore...
