I'm not really a “motion designer” by trade. However, I feel like the concepts that are behind much of what is in designed in motion apply when something is static. Also, the concepts of interaction, cause-and-effect, and even sequence are all required in web and print design—these are key in motion design as well.

My biggest pet peeve in motion projects is that typically the motion used just isn't really necessary other than as a parlor trick. It adds a pretty something else, but that often gets in the way of the actual meaning or message to begin with.

NOTES FROM MOGRAPH STUDENTS THEMSELVES
++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

Unlike a traditional animation, motion graphics address animating for the sake of movement, not for the sake of a story...

the focus is to make something move in an aesthetically pleasing or interesting manner.

motion graphics focus on giving a typically still image or type a character to make it more engaging

motion graphics are often more a carrier of information

a motion graphic might use the motion of a cat applied to a logo to show a playful elegance or personality of a company, but it is still motion for motion's sake.

but motion graphics tend to be relatively short...

Once there is a finite definition of "graphic design" then the definition of "motion graphics" will simply be "graphic design in motion"

Anything that was designed with motion in mind, or that will have to be visualized while in motion.

a change over time using the principles of design...

motion graphics isn't film, it isn't animation, but it can have those as part of it...

motion graphics is a set of designed elements that use frame-by-frame footage to create a change in those elements over time — does this have to be frame-by-frame???

motion graphics can just be defined as dynamic graphic design.

Motion graphics brings the visual to life? (or at least tries to)

It has the ability to flat graphics into a 3D sense of space
it adds the dimension of time to create a sense of linear form???
