¶2
————

In conceptual art the idea or concept is the most important aspect of the work
    - is graphic design like this?

How does Lewitt describe the Conceptual Art process?
… all the planning and decisions are made before hand and that the execution is a perfunctory affair

perfunctory defn: Carried out with a minimum of effort or reflection; characterized by routine or superficiality : mechanical; lacking in interest or enthusiasm

"The idea becomes the machine that makes the art"

Purposeless

free from dependence on skill

mentally interesting, emotionally dry.

¶7
————

to work with a plan that is preset is one way of avoiding subjectivity

obviates the necessity of designing each work in turn — the plan would design the work.

obviate defn:  1. Remove (a need or difficulty): "the Venetian blinds obviated the need for curtains". 2. Avoid; prevent: "a parachute can obviate disaster". — to anticipate and prevent (as a situation) or make unnecessary (as an action).

the fewer decisions made during the course of the completing the work, the better.
    - is there a reason that this would be good/useful in a design practice (or in GD in general)?
