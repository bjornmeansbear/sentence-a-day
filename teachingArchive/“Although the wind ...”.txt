Pick 20 images that you feel go with the text.

You will interpret the poem through the images in two ways.

Make a video that uses those 20 images in some sort of sequence, the text from the poem, and any other graphics you create on top. You can use each chosen image only once (you must keep transitioning...)

Make a booklet that reinterprets your sequence still using the 20 images, the text from the poem, and any other graphics you create on top. Bring this printed booklet to class next week. You can use your images as many times as you need.

Spend no more than 3 hours on each.
