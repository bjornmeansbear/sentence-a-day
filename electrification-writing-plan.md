# Electrification writing plan

Started 2026-10-27. A checklist for bridging the wiki's theory (commons, F/LOS, vernacular, adhocism, circular economy) to the hands-on work happening now: the solar rig, the e-bike, radiant heat, PPTC. And a hedge. Every item here builds a public record that stays useful whether I stay at MICA or leave.

## How to use this

- **Notion pings, this file answers.** Each Notion task has a due date. When the alert fires, open this file, find the matching week, do that one thing.
- One small item a day. One essay a month. Check the box when it exists, even rough.
- Build first, argue second. People trust the argument after they watch the hands.
- One idea, many formats. Every essay becomes a carousel, a reel script, and a Climate Designers post. That's CAPE. I already named the system.
- To get a head start, tell Claude: "skeleton [title]". To get a draft to react to: "draft [title]". Expect to rewrite it.

**Tags for new work:** `Electrification`, plus `Build Log`, `Object`, `Definition`, or `Question` as fits.

**Kinds of item**

- `note` — a definition or reference tiddler, 50 to 150 words
- `object` — an `Object` entry: what it is, who made it, what it's made of, one line on what it argues
- `log` — a `Build Log` entry: dated, photographed, honest about what failed
- `essay` — 500 to 1,000 words, starting from a concrete thing
- `post` — Instagram/YouTube. Captions on every video, alt text on every image, contrast checked (AA)
