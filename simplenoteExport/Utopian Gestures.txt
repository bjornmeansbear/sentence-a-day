Or, what does sustainable graphic design look like?

My work over the last 15 years hasn’t always exactly been “design” but, it has always been about environmental and social concern.

Let me start with a question that I posed to myself while in graduate school: what does sustainable gd look like?

I know what modernism looks like
I know what futurism looks like
What romanticism looks like
What the renaissance looks like...
What’s sustainism as a “look?”

In looking to artists and designers from previous movements, innovators we document are known for novel visuals arrived at through exploring new ideologic territory and experimenting with different ways of thinking. Avant garde visuals stemming from these movements were the result of the ideas (or ideals) to come out of their novel thinking, not the main ideas themselves. Our art historical past is not about style for style’s sake, but style that illuminates a theoretical position (or at least style that is arrived at from a thought process, from a set of values, or from some set of hypotheses). 

Now I could go into a whole thing just about how forms become departed from their ideologies... 

So, what does sustainable look like?

It could look the same? Basically look like any other neo liberal capitalist tchotchke but be “better” 

It could look eco friendly and wear its green heart on its sleeve

It could look innovative and show how different it is through looking new, trchy, special, whatever

Or, maybe... it looks like nothing — maybe it doesn’t exist???
