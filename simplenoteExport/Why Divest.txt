Why Divest.

The overarching “fossil fuel divestment” that say, 350org requests, is really about not owning any stocks of companies that do extraction or processing of fossil fuels - coal. oil. Natural gas… ESG takes that into account, and has some other more possible social issues it also tries to consider… 

BP, Shell and Total are all oil companies that in the last few weeks have said they are no longer focusing on oil, and will instead invest in other diversified energies.

GM will phase out all internal combustion engines by 2035, the other car makers are poised to make this declaration too as most European and asian countries are creating strict laws to this regard anyway… 

BlackRock, the largest investment company on earth, has said as of January that they see Climate Change views as a key issue for "creating durable value" in a company -- so, companies that DON'T consider Climate Change, well they won't be considered by the BlackRock investment managers... If BlackRock doesn't think its fiduciary-ly responsible to not account for climate in investments, then why does the board? And this isn't environmentalist or socialist, it is not because they really think it is good or bad for the planet or for people, its a financial decision, it isn't fiscally sensical to invest in oil and gas and such.

Bank of America, Wells Fargo, Goldman Sachs, JP Morgan Chase, and Citigroup all have said that they WILL NOT be investing in oil searches, at least not on any of the new USA land opened up...
